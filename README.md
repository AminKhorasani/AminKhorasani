## About me

I build at the intersection of **data, analytics, AI, and product** — from business intelligence and experimentation to backend systems and user-facing products.

My work usually lives somewhere between turning messy data into decisions, designing analytical systems, and shipping software that people can actually use.

---

## What I build

- **Data & Analytics** — business intelligence, KPI systems, experimentation, dashboards, decision frameworks
- **AI & Data Products** — intelligent workflows, analytics agents, automation, data-centric product experiences
- **Backend Systems** — APIs, data models, service architecture, databases, production-minded tooling
- **Full-stack Products** — from idea and product logic to working web applications
